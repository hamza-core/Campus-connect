# Categories and departments

## How routing works
Every complaint belongs to one of four categories. Each category is handled by one department, and the complaint reaches that department automatically.

## Cafeteria
Handled by **Cafeteria Administration**. Typical issues: poor food quality or hygiene; high food prices; limited food variety; unprofessional staff behaviour or poor service.

## Library
Handled by **Library Administration**. Typical issues: insufficient or outdated books and learning resources; librarian availability or staff behaviour; library opening and closing hours; insufficient seating or study space.

## Labs & IT
Handled by **Laboratory / IT Administration**. Typical issues: non-functional computers; internet connectivity issues; projector or multimedia equipment problems; missing or unavailable software.

## Teaching & Academic
Handled by **Teaching Manager / Academic Coordination**. Typical issues: teachers arriving late to lectures; unavailability of lecture notes or learning materials; inadequate explanation of course topics; classroom management or discipline issues.

## Choosing a category
Pick the category of the service the problem is about. If a problem touches two areas, submit one complaint for each so each department sees its part.
