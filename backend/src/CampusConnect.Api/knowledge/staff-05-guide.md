# Guide for department and campus administrators

## Department administrator
You see only your own department's complaints, in two tabs: **Current** (Pending and In progress) and **History** (Resolved and Rejected). Open a complaint to read the details. You can start work, write a remark, and mark it Resolved or Rejected. A reason is required to resolve or reject. If another administrator changed the complaint at the same moment you will be asked to reload before saving.

## Super administrator
You see every complaint and the campus dashboards. The role is read-only: you can search and filter but not change a complaint.

## Filters and search
Lists can be searched by title, ticket or description, and filtered by status, category, department (super administrator), priority, and **Overdue only**. The Export button shares the filtered list as a CSV file.

## Dashboards
The dashboard shows totals per status, the number of overdue complaints, the average resolution time and average rating, a status chart, a 14-day trend, and the most frequent recurring issues. The super administrator also sees a bar chart comparing departments. Numbers refresh automatically.

## Reading the numbers
- **Overdue**: open complaints past their deadline.
- **Average resolution**: average time from submission to resolution of resolved complaints.
- **Average rating**: mean of the 1 to 5 student ratings.
- **Recurring issues**: the problems reported most often by category and issue type.
