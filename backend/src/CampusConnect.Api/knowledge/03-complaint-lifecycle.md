# Complaint lifecycle

## Statuses
- **Pending**: the complaint was received and is waiting for the department.
- **In progress**: the department has started working on it.
- **Resolved**: the department fixed or answered it. A reply explains what was done.
- **Rejected**: the department decided not to act on it. A reply explains the reason.
Resolved and Rejected are final: the status does not change after that. If the problem comes back, submit a new complaint and mention the earlier ticket number.

## Who changes the status
Only the administrator of the department that owns the complaint can change its status or reply. Students and the Super Administrator cannot change a complaint.

## Deadlines and overdue complaints
Each complaint has a response deadline that depends on its priority: High priority 24 hours, Medium priority 72 hours (3 days), Low priority 7 days. If a complaint is still Pending or In progress after its deadline it is marked **Overdue** so the department can see it needs attention. Choose High priority only for urgent problems.

## Replies and the timeline
When a department resolves or rejects a complaint it must write a reason. Replies appear on the complaint in the "Department remarks" section, and every step is listed in the Timeline with its date.

## Editing or deleting
Once submitted, a complaint cannot be edited or deleted in the app. If you made a mistake, submit a new complaint with the correct details.

## Ratings
Resolved complaints can be rated once from 1 to 5 stars, with an optional comment. Ratings help the university see how well departments respond.
