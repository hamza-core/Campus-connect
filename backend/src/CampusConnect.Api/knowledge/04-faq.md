# Frequently asked questions

## I forgot my password. What do I do?
The app does not have a password reset feature yet. Contact the university IT support or the Laboratory / IT Administration to have your account helped, or register a new student account with another email address.

## Why can't I see another student's complaint?
Complaints are private. Students see only their own complaints, and department staff see only the complaints of their department.

## Can administrators see my name?
Department administrators see your name unless you chose **Hide my name from department staff** when you submitted. The Super Administrator can always see it.

## How long until I get a response?
Departments should respond within the deadline for the priority you chose: 24 hours for High, 72 hours for Medium, 7 days for Low. You are notified as soon as the status changes or a reply is written.

## Why was my complaint rejected?
The department writes the reason in its reply on the complaint. If you disagree or the problem continues, submit a new complaint with more detail and mention the earlier ticket number.

## Can I attach photos?
Yes, up to 3 photos of up to 5 MB each. They are uploaded and attached to your complaint. Also describe the problem in the text, and the AI can suggest the issue from your photo.

## I did not get a notification.
Check that notifications are allowed for CampusConnect in your phone settings and that battery saver is not restricting the app. The status is always visible in the Complaints list even without a notification.

## Is CampusConnect for emergencies?
No. For emergencies or anything involving immediate danger, contact campus security or the emergency services directly. CampusConnect is for facility and service problems that departments can resolve.

## What if my problem does not fit a category?
Choose the category closest to the service involved. If none fits, describe the problem clearly in the description of the nearest category.

## Can I submit the same problem twice?
Avoid duplicates: first check your Complaints list for an existing ticket. If the problem is already reported by you and still open, wait for the response or raise the priority in a new complaint only if it became urgent.
