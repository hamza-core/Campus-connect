# About CampusConnect

## What CampusConnect is
CampusConnect is the campus complaint and feedback system. Students report problems with university facilities and services, the complaint is sent automatically to the responsible department, and the student can follow its progress and read the department's replies.

## Who uses it
- **Students** register, log in, submit complaints, follow their status, read department replies, and rate resolved complaints. A student sees only their own complaints.
- **Department administrators** (Cafeteria, Library, Laboratory/IT, Teaching/Academic) see only the complaints of their own department. They start work, resolve or reject complaints, and write replies.
- **The Super Administrator** can see every complaint and the campus-wide statistics, but cannot change complaints (read-only).
Administrator accounts are created by the university; they cannot be registered in the app.

## How to submit a complaint
1. Tap the **+** button in the bottom navigation.
2. Choose the category (Cafeteria, Library, Labs & IT, Teaching & Academic).
3. Optionally pick the kind of problem, then write a short **title** (5 to 120 characters) and a **description** (at least 10 characters).
4. Optionally add a location, choose a priority (Low, Medium, High), attach up to 3 photos (the AI then suggests the issue), and choose to hide your name from department staff.
5. Tap **Submit complaint**. You get a ticket number such as CC-2026-0042.
You do not choose the department: CampusConnect sends the complaint to the right one from the category.

## Photos
You can attach up to 3 photos (JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB each). Photos are uploaded to the CampusConnect server and attached to your complaint. Still describe the problem in words so staff can act on it quickly.

## AI photo suggestion
When you add a photo, an AI looks at it and suggests the kind of problem, a short issue label, and a priority. You see an "AI suggests" card and choose whether to use it; you can always pick the issue yourself. The list of problem types comes from the issues students reported most often in that category, so it improves over time. If the AI is busy or unavailable, choose the issue manually.

## Following a complaint
Open **Complaints** in the bottom navigation to see your complaints, filter by status, and search. Open a complaint to see its details, the department's replies, and a timeline of every step. The lists refresh by themselves every few seconds.

## Notifications
You receive a notification when a department changes the status of your complaint or writes a reply. Notifications appear live in the app (the bell icon on the Home screen), and as a system notification when the app is minimized. On Android you must allow notifications the first time you sign in.

## Privacy
Only you and the responsible department can open your complaint. If you choose **Hide my name from department staff**, the department sees the complaint without your name; the Super Administrator can still see it.

## Ratings
After a complaint is **Resolved**, you can rate how it was handled from 1 to 5 stars and add a comment. You can rate each complaint once.
